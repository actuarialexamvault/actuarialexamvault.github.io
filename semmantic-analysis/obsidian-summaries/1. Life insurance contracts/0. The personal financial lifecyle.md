> [!grad] **Ages 16 – 25 (Young Adult):** Future goals & debt settlement
>Individuals in this stage may still be in higher education or starting their first job, often with parental support and few dependants. Their primary financial needs include **saving for future goals** like home ownership, **paying off student debts**, and funding leisure activities.
>- Generally zero demand of life insurance products due to lack of dependants and or may still be covered under parents life insurance.
>- While there is a need for savings towards goals like a home purchase, life insurance contracts are typically too long-term to serve this group effectively.

> [!family] **Ages 25 – 35 (Family Formation):** Protecting dependants
This period is characterized by the acquisition of **large debts** (such as mortgages), moderate income, and high expenditure related to raising children. The immediate need is **protecting dependants** in the event of an earner's **death or illness**, and far-sighted individuals may begin **saving for retirement**.
>
> Demanded\recommended products:
> - [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/1. Life insurance contracts/2.2 Term assurances|Term assurance]]: there is a high demand for affordable temporary cover to protect dependants from death, sickness and disability.
> - [[Decreasing term assurance]]: for repaying home-loans on the death of the main bread-winner.
> - [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/1. Life insurance contracts/2.3 Convertible or renewable term assurances|Convertible or renewable term assurance]]: attractive for individuals with uncertain future needs, as they offer guarantees for continuation and options to convert to permanent contracts without further medical proof.
> - [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/1. Life insurance contracts/4.1 Unit-linked contracts|Unit-linked products]]: suitable for those who are risk-tolerant and want flexibility to vary benefit levels as their needs evolve.
> - [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/2. Health and care products/5.1 Income Protection (IP) contracts|Income protection]]: To protect against loss of income from to incapacity to work due to illness or disability. 
> - [[Group Contract|Group life benefits]]:  this is an ideal time for employer-sponsored group life insurance, including protection and retirement savings products.

> [!saving] **Ages 35 – 65 (Mid-life Adult):** Saving for retirement
During this stage, children typically become independent and debts are paid off, often leading to a surplus of income over expenditure. The **biggest financial need is saving for retirement**, though individuals also focus on providing for **future long-term care** and managing the **transfer of wealth** to the next generation.
> 

> [!retirement] **Ages Over 65 (Retirement):** Managing wealth and long-term care
As individuals move from employment to retirement, they generally have lower income but more accumulated wealth. The main risks at this stage are **running out of money** if they live to a very old age and an **increasingly imminent need for long-term care**

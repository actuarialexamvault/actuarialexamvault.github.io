
> [!NOTE] [[Surrender Value]]
> - Surrender values are typically available.
> - Surrender values for [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/1. Life insurance contracts/1.1 Endowment assurances|endowment assurances]] usually increase over the term of the contract.
> - Does not have to be related to the benefit payable on death or maturity e.g. return a portion of the premiums paid.

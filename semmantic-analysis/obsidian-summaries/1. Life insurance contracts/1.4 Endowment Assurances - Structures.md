
> [!ABSTRACT] [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/1. Life insurance contracts/1.1 Endowment assurances]] - Structures
> **Without-profits**: 
> - Offers a guaranteed amount of money at the end of the term or upon earlier death in exchange for single or regular premiums
> 
> **With-profits (Participating)**: 
> - The initial sum assured is enhanced by the declaration of bonuses (reversionary, special or terminal). Policyholders expect better returns than without-profits version, though premiums are typically higher.
> 
> **Unit-linked**: 
> - Premiums are invested in pooled funds, and the maturity benefit depends on the performance of underlying assets and the level of charges levied by the insurer. Highly versatile, allowing policyholders to choose between protection-heavy or savings-heavy designs therefore can be used to meet different needs.

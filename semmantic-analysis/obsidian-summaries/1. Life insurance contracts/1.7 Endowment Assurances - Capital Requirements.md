
> [!solvency] [[../../Definitions/Capital requirements]]
> How much initial capital is needed to write a contract depends on a number of factors.
> 
> | Factor Affecting Capital | **Endowment assurances** |
> | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
> | **Contract Design** | **High.** Designed as savings vehicles, they require large reserves to meet guaranteed maturity benefits. |
> | **Initial Expenses** | **High.** Significant acquisition costs (commissions, marketing) often exceed the first premium, creating "new business strain". |
> | **Solvency Capital Requirements** | **Moderate.** Focused on matching assets to liabilities and protecting against market fluctuations. |
> | **Reserving vs. Pricing Basis** | **Significant.** If the reserving basis is stronger (more prudent) than the pricing basis, capital is tied up from the start. |
> | **Premium Frequency** | **Variable.** Single premiums require the least capital; regular monthly premiums require the most. |


> [!INFO]  Customer needs
> Protection:
> - Against loss of income (long-term security for dependants)
> - Amount of wealth transfer on early death
> - Funeral expenses
> - Tax liability (duties) and tax efficiency (depending on tax regime)
> 
> Whole life contracts are primarily seen as **protection contracts**

> [!TIP] Cases where whole life assurance functions as a savings vehicle
> Despite their **protective nature**, they can be used as a savings vehicle:
> - Saving for known future outgo
> 	- funeral costs
> 	- wealth transfer & accumulation
> 	- Tax-efficient wealth transfer
> - With-profits and unit linked variations: When structured as with-profits or unit linked, the policy acts as a long-term investment where the eventual pay-out is enhanced by bonuses or investment returns.

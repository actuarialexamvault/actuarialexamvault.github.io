
> [!NOTE] [[Surrender Value]]
> - A surrender value could be placed on a [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/1. Life insurance contracts/2.1 Whole life assurances|whole life assurance]] although recent market practice in South Africa is not to offer surrender benefit.
> - If a surrender value is available it will usually increase over time.
> - There may also be a "[[Paid-Up Policy]]" option (also not common in SA)

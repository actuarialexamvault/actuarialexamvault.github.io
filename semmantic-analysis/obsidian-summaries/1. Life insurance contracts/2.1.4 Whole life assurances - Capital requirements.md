
> [!solvency] [[../../Definitions/Capital requirements]]
> Factors affecting capital requirements of [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/1. Life insurance contracts/2.1 Whole life assurances|whole life assurances]]
> 
> | Factor Affecting Capital | Impact |
> | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
> | Contract Design | **High.** Similar to endowments; they have no fixed term and build significant reserves over long durations. |
> | Initial Expenses | **High.** Capital requirements are similar to endowments due to high setup and commission costs. |
> | [[Solvency Margin]] | **Moderate.** Similar to endowments; must hold a "cushion" for both investment and mortality volatility. |
> | Reserving vs. Pricing Basis | **Significant.** The difference between realistic expected mortality/interest and the prudent reserving basis drives capital needs. |
> | Premium Frequency | **Variable.** Similar to endowments; regular premiums lead to negative initial asset shares. |

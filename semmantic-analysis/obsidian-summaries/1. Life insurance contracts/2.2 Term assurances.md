
> [!CITE] Term assurance
> A contract to pay a benefit on the death of the life insured **only if** the death occurs within the  term of the contract (chosen at outset). There is usually no payment if the policyholder survives until the end of the term.
>
***Benefits***:
>- fixed lump sum or
>- [[Decreasing term assurance|decreasing lump sum]]
>- regular income for the remainder of the term
>
***Premiums***:
>- Single premium
>- Level or increasing regular premiums 
>- Guaranteed or reviewable. 
>- Low/affordable premiums 
>
>***Structural basis***: Typically written on a conventional without-profit basis, although with-profits, unit-linked  and index-linked versions could be offered.

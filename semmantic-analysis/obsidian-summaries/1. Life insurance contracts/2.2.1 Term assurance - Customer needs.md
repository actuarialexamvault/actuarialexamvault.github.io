
> [!info] Customer needs met by term assurances
> Protection
>- ***Individuals***: protects **dependants** from financial loss upon the death of a breadwinner. *Decreasing term assurance* can be used to repay the outstanding balance of a loan/mortgage.
>- ***Corporate/Keyman***: Businesses use it to protect against the loss of a **key-person** whose death could delay projects or lead to loss of business.
>- ***Group schemes***: Employers often provide term assurances to staff as a benefit payable upon **death in service**. It can also be used by credit card companies to **[[Indemnity|indemnify]] balances** if a cardholder dies.

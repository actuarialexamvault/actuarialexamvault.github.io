> [!summary] Capital requirements for term assurances
> The following factors affect capital requirements for term assurance contracts:
>
> - **Product design**: Very Low. Protection-only nature means reserves tend to be small throughout the contract.
> - **Initial Expenses**: High. High levels of medical underwriting and initial commissions create large strain relative to premium income.
> - **Solvency Margin**: High. Insurers may need large margins to withstand extreme events, such as an epidemic occurring once every 200 years.
> - **Reserving vs. Pricing Basis**: Low Reserve Impact. Because reserves are small, the focus is more on the solvency margin than the base reserve strength.
> - **Premium Frequency**: Moderate to High. Regular premium contracts have high strain because only one installment is received against high initial costs.

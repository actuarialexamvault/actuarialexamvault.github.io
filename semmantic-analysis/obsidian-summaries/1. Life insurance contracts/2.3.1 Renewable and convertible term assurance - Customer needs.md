
> [!info] Customer needs met by [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/1. Life insurance contracts/2.3 Convertible or renewable term assurances|Convertible or renewable term assurances]]
> **Affordability and Flexibility:** 
> - These contracts are attractive because they provide **low-cost death cover** while offering the certainty of being able to switch to permanent protection (whole life or endowment) later when the policyholder can better afford the higher premiums.

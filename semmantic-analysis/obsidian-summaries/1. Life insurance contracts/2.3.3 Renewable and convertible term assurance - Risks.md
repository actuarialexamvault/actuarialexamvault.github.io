
> [!skull] [[Risks to the insurer]]
> - **Heightened Anti-selection:** The primary risk is a significant increase in **[[Anti-selection]]** at the date of exercise. 
> 	- **Renewal vs. Conversion Risk:** The option to **renew** typically poses a **greater risk** to the insurer than the option to convert. This is because term assurance remains the cheapest way to maximize a death benefit; a person in poor health will prefer to renew a low-cost term policy rather than convert to an expensive endowment or whole life policy.
> 	- **Managing Option Costs:** Insurers may mitigate the expected losses from these options by charging an **extra premium** throughout the original contract term. Furthermore, in some markets, certain tests (such as an AIDS test) may still be allowed at the point of renewal


> [!solvency] Capital & reserving requirements for renewable and convertible term assurances
> Capital requirements for these products are generally **higher than for basic [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/1. Life insurance contracts/2.2 Term assurances]]** because of the need for additional reserves for [[Option (Health)|options]] and guarantees.
>
>
>![[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Diagrams/Pasted image 20260114150425.png]]
>- **Initial Uncertainty:** At the start of the contract, higher reserves or solvency capital may be required due to the **uncertainty and risk** associated with future costs of the options are exercised.
>
>- **End-of-Term Preparation:** As the renewal date approaches, the insurer must accumulate sufficient reserves to fund the **increased mortality costs** of the "sub-standard" lives expected to exercise the renewal option
>

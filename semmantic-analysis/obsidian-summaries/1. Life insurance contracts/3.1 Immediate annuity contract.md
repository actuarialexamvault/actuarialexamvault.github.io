
> [!definition] Immediate annuity
> A contract to pay out regular amounts of benefit provided the insured life is alive at the time of payment. The term immediate implies that payments start right away without a deferred period.
> 
> Benefits
>- level or variable
>- linked to inflation (to protect against erosion of benefits by inflation)
>
>Premiums
>- Single premium (usually from accumulated pension fund)

> [!EXAMPLE] Available structures on annuity contracts
>- conventional without-profits (guaranteed income at outset)
>- index-linked (e.g. linked to an inflation index)
>- with-profits (income fluctuates with bonuses declaired)
>- unit-linked (income fluctuates with unit prices)

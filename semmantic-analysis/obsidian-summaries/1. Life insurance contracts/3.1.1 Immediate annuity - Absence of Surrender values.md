
> [!missing] Surrender values
> Surrender values are **normally not available** due to the **high risk of anti-selection**: 
>- A policyholder is most likely to want to surrender if they suspect they are in severe ill-health and will not receive many future payments.
>- However it is possible to transfer an annuity in payment to a different provider.
>- In some jurisdictions it is possible to sell an annuity in payment in a secondary market and thus convert the outstanding regular benefit into a cash lump sum.
>- The payments would contractually continue to be dependant on the insured life, therefore the purchaser would need to assess the health of the policyholder and will pay substantially less to those in ill-health.


> [!INFO] Customer needs 
> Protection:
> - ***Lifetime income***: The primary goal is to convert capital into a guaranteed income, removing the uncertainty of how quickly a consumer should spend their capital.
> - ***Longevity protection***: it protects individuals from the risk of living too long and running out of money, thereby protecting their future standard of living.
> - ***Compulsory Purchase***: In some jurisdictions, pension legislation may require individuals to purchase an annuity using their accumulated pension funds at retirement.
> - ***Flexibility***: Immediate annuities can be payable for temporary periods only, making them suitable for example to pay school fees for children.
> - ***Protecting dependants***: Joint life annuities, particularly those written on a last survivor basis, address the need to provide an income for dependants following the death of the main life.

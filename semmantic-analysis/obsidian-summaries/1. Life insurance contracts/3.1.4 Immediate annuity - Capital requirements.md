>
>| Factor Affecting Capital          | Annuity contracts                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Contract Design**               | **Moderate to High.** Immediate annuities require substantial reserves to meet long-term lifetime income.                                   |
| **Initial Expenses**              | **Low to Moderate.** Usually single premium, so setup costs are covered immediately, though strain can still arise from reserving rules.    |
| **Solvency Capital Requirements** | **High.** Longevity risk (policyholders living longer than expected) is harder to predict and requires greater prudence.                    |
| **Reserving vs. Pricing Basis**   | **Critical.** Small differences between pricing and valuation interest rates (e.g., 5.5% vs. 4%) create massive capital shortfalls on sale. |
| **Premium Frequency**             | **Low.** Most are single premium, meaning the asset share is heavily positive at inception, reducing (though not eliminating) strain.       |

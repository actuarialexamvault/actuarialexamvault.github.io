
> [!definition] Definition
> A **deferred annuity** is a contract designed to provide regular benefit payments starting at a future **"vesting" date**, provided the life insured is alive at that time and at subsequent payment dates

> [!note] Premiums
> - These contracts can be purchased through **single premiums** or, more commonly, **regular premiums** payable up to the vesting date.
> - Single premiums offer flexibility, as a consumer may choose to take out a new policy each year rather than committing to level annual/month payments. ([[Why would a consumer choose to take out a new policy each year rather than pay level annual payments]]?)
> 

> [!info] Structures
> They may be written on a **without-profits**, **unit linked** or **with-profits** basis. In a with-profits deferred annuity, **bonuses** are added to the guaranteed income level during the deferment period.
>
>**Alternative**: Endowment assurance plus immediate annuity
>
>- A similar financial goal can be met by combining an **endowment assurance** with an **immediate annuity** that begins at the endowment's maturity. 
>- However, this combined structure may involve higher expenses, such as double commission, and introduces uncertainty regarding future annuity rates

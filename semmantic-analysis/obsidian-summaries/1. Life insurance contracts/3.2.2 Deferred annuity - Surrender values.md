
>[!TIP] Surrender value
>- Surrender values are **not normally available post vesting** but may be **payable during the deferred period**. 
>- Where the deferred annuity is an investment to provide a **pension**, legislation is likely to require that the surrender value be **reinvested** in a pension arrangement rather than being paid directly to the insured.

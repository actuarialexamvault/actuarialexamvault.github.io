
> [!solvency] Unit fund
> The bid value of the policyholders unit fund at any point in time is the amount of money that the company would pay to the policyholder on a claim under the policy (on death, maturity or surrender) ignoring any guaranteed minimum benefit or the effect of surrender penalties that may be imposed.

> [!money-tree] Non-unit fund
>- The non-unit fund is a **unified account** where the total pool of charges is used to meet the total pool of expenses.
>- It is made up of:
>	- the accumulated value of all the charges the company has taken out of its unit-linked policies, 
>	- less: all the actual costs it has incurred on behalf of those contracts (expenses & claim costs for guaranteed sum assureds above the unit fund amount)
>	- less: any distributions of profit it has made to its providers of capital
>	- plus: any capital injections paid in
>- Profit is derived from the **net results** of the entire non-unit fund: the sum of expense charges minus actual expenses, plus the sum of risk charges minus the actual cost of providing guaranteed benefits.


> [!info] [[Customer needs]]
>- ***Affordability***: Consumers obtain a **higher expected level of benefit** for a given premium or pay a **lower expected premium** for a given benefit [[Unit-linked contract benefits compared to non-linked versions|compared to non-linked versions]].
>- Flexibility is build into the design:
>	- ***Flexibility of Investment choice***: Consumers can choose from a range of investment funds that align with their risk appetite and objectives.
>	- **Flexibility of Premium structure & frequency**: Customers can vary premiums or even miss premium payments for ad-hoc periods without lapsing or being made paid up immediately. 
>	- **Flexibility in the type & levels of cover**: Policyholders can also select and change the desired mix or savings and protection as their [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/1. Life insurance contracts/0. The personal financial lifecyle|life circumstances]] evolve.
>- ***Inflation protection***: Consumers can choose investments in real assets like equities and property which can provide real returns and protect against the erosion of benefits by inflation.
>- ***Savings***: They can be used as vehicles for **retirement savings** or as a means to **repay the capital on interest only-loans**.
>- ***Wealth transfer***: the can be used to transfer wealth to the next generation (similar to endowments).
>- ***Group benefits***: employers utilize unit-linked [[Group Contract|group contracts]] to provide retirement and death-in-service benefits to employees.

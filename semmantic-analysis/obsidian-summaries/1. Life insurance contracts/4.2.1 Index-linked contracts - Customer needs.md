
> [!info] Customer needs
>- ***Inflation protection***: protects against the **erosion of the real value of benefits caused by inflation** by linking benefits to an economic index, such as a retail price index. This effectively provides a *guaranteed zero real rate of return*, which can be more attractive than other investments during periods of **high inflation**.
>- ***Standard of living:*** Products like *index-linked annuities* address the need for a lifetime income that is shielded from inflation, to protect the standard of living as costs rise.

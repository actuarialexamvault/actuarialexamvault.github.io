
> [!trending-down] Investment risk
> The main risk of [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/1. Life insurance contracts/4.2. Index-linked contracts|index-linked contracts]] is investment risk.
>- ***Practical matching difficulties***: it may be impractical or too expensive to invest so as to match precisely the benefit guarantee it is giving.
>-  ***Mismatching risk***: Since approximate matching is used, the risk that the assets held do not move in line with the investment or economic index is borne by the insurer. 
>- ***Investment strategy risk***: if the insurer adopts an investment strategy to achieve higher real returns such as mixing equities with index-linked government securities rather than a 100% match, the company is exposed to the risk of making investment losses.


> [!question] What is a passive valuation approach?
> A valuation methodology that is relatively **insensitive to changes in market conditions**
>- Assumptions are locked-in at the time the policy is first written
>- Assets are often valued at historic cost or book value
>- Solvency capital requirements are typically [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/11. Capital requirements/2. Calculating SCR#^5db954|simple formulas]]

> [!explainer] For active approach see [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/10. Reserving/6. Market-consistent valuation|Market-consistent valuation]]

>[!exchange]  Compare Active vs Passive valuation methods
>
>|Feature|Passive Valuation|Active Valuation|
>|---|---|---|
>|**Market Sensitivity**|Relatively insensitive|Closely tied to market conditions|
>|**Update Frequency**|Updated infrequently|Updated frequently (often daily)|
>|**Assumptions**|Often "locked in" at inception|Market-consistent assumptions|
>|**Asset Valuation**|Historic cost or "book value"|Current market value|
>|**Liability Valuation**|Net premium method|Risk-free discount rates|
>|**Solvency Capital**|Simple formulas (e.g., fixed % of liabilities)|Risk-based capital (e.g., VaR)|


> [!definition] What is a market-consistent valuation?
> In modern solvency frameworks like Solvency II, the fundamental principle that assets and liabilities should be valued at [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Definitions/Fair Value|Fair Value]]. Under this approach assets are valued at market price and liabilities are valued by discounting projected cashflows at risk-free interest rates.

> [!cycle] Why is the MCV considered an active valuation approach?
> - MCV requires frequent updates to valuation assumptions
> - Highly sensitive to market changes
> - Requires [[8. Risk-neutral calibration|risk-neutral calibration]] of stochastic models

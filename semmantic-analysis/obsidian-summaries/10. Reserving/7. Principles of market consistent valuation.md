
> [!money-tree] How are assets valued under MCV?
>- Assets are valued at **market price**

> [!solvency] How are liabilities valued under MCV?
> - Replicating portfolio market price is used.
>- Liability cashflows are valued using **risk-free discount rates**
> - Rates are derived from government bonds or swaps
> - Deductions may be applied for **credit risk**
> - Discount rates are independent of actual assets held

> [!trending-up] What is the illiquidity premium in MCV?
> An illiquidity premium is added to the risk-free rates for long-term **predictable liabilities** where the matching assets are **held to maturity**.

> [!skull] How does MCV value non-market risks?
>- Best estimate assumptions are used with an explicit risk margin added
>- Cost of capital approach is used for risk margin calculation


> [!definition] What is risk-neutral calibration?
> A method of setting stochastic model parameters so the model replicates observed market prices of actual financial instruments as closely as possible.

> [!gears] How is a stochastic model calibrated using risk-neutral calibration?
>1. **Select** **benchmark financial instruments** (swaps, bonds, options) with known market prices
>2. Project cashflows for these instruments across **randomly simulated scenarios** and **adjust model parameters** until the **average present value** of simulated outcomes is sufficiently close to market prices.
>3. The market calibrated parameters are plugged into an **economic scenario generator (ESG)** that produces thousands of possible future paths for interest rates, equity returns, and inflation
>4. For each ESG scenario, project the liability cashflows and discount them to calculate the average liability which gives you a **market consistent value of  liabilities**. 

> [!explainer] Risk neutral calibration is possible because of the [[Martingale property]]

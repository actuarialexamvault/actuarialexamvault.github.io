
> [!solvency] What are Solvency Capital Requirements (SCR)?
> The minimum level of assets are life insurance company must maintain in addition to its mathematical reserves

> [!question] What is the purpose of the SCR?
> To provide an extra layer of protection for policyholders, ensuring that guarantees are met even if reserves are underestimated or asset values drop.

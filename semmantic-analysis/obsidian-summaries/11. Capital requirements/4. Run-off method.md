
>[!why] What is the run-off method?
>A capital calculation approach determining the assets needed at the valuation date to cover existing liabilities until the **very last policy expires**

> [!lapse] How is capital calculated using the run-off method?
> 
>
>- Projects assets and liabilities **until the last in force policy terminates** 
>- Incorporates suitable stresses (shocks) to risk factors
>- Ensures assets always exceed liabilities
>- throughout the entire projection period
>- Ignores future new business

> [!question] Why would an insurer choose to use the run-off method? 
> - To guarantee a the firm has sufficient capital at the start to ensure assets always exceed liabilities throughout the entire lifetime of the contracts
> - To provide a long-term view of solvency that accounts for extreme stresses over decades rather than just 12 months.


> [!question] List the principles for determining surrender values
>  **P.R.A.C.T.I.C.E.S.** **Fairness**
> - **Policyholders reasonable expectations**
> 	- Influenced by the company's past practice and literature
> -  **Risk and Regulations**
> 	- Avoid selection against the insurer
> 	- Prevent lapse and re-entry risk
> 	- Immediate revision of terms following market crashes
> 	- Comply with regulations
> 
>  - **Asset share constraints**
> 	- Should not pay more than the asset share on average
> 	- Ensures the company doesn't make a financial loss
> 	- Basing the surrender value on the full asset share means that the company will not make a profit on surrender
> - **Competition**
> 	- be comparable to surrender values offered by competitors
>
>- **Timing** - **Later durations**
> 	- Consistency with projected maturity values
> 	- Ensure smooth progression of values as maturity approaches
>- **Calculation and communication**
> 	- Simple calculation methods given computing power available
> 	- Clear documentation and explanation
>- **Early durations**
> 	- Asset share is negative because of initial expenses, the company cannot avoid losses because a negative surrender value is not possible. This can be addressed by increasing cross-subsidies between late surrenders and early surrenders
> 	- Avoid values appearing too low relative to premiums paid
> 	- Take into account any projections and illustrations given at issue
> - **Stability of surrender values**
> 	- They should progress smoothly over duration and the method/basis should not change frequently, unless justified by harsh financial conditions
> - **Fairness** 
> 	- Surrender values should maintain fairness between:
> 		- policyholders leaving and staying
> 		- policyholders and shareholders
>

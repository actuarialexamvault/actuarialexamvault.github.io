
>[!question] What is the main principle when calculating terms for general alterations?
>The main principle of the equating policy values method is that the **reserve (or policy value)** held before the alteration must equal the **prospective reserve** of the altered policy plus any **administrative costs** of the alteration.

> [!definition] How is the basis for the equating policy values method determined?
> Generally what is used are the assumptions to price a new contract on the date the alteration occurs.

> [!math] When to use select mortality in an alteration?
> Select mortality is used if the alteration requires new underwriting evidence (like increasing the sum assured). If no evidence is required, ultimate mortality is more appropriate. 

> [!best-estimate] How does the basis affect the expected profit from altered contracts?
>
The insurer chooses between best estimate or prudent assumptions (incorporating margins) prevailing at the date of the alteration
>
>The profit released on the original contract:
> - Using a best estimate (realistic) basis for the "before" value **releases all expected future profit** from the original policy immediately
> - Using the current premium basis (with margins) for the "before" value **allows profit to emerge gradually** over the remaining term.
>
>The profit expected to emerge over the remaining life of the altered contract will be:
> - **no profit at all**, if a **realistic prospective value** is used for the policy value after alteration
> - profit corresponding to the **margins in the assumptions**, if a prospective value using a **prudent basis** (with margins) is used for the policy value after alteration.
>
>The company will use a combination of bases depending on the profit that it wishes to retain. Companies use a **blended basis** that starts with premium basis assumptions (with margins) and transitions toward best-estimate assumptions (realistic) as the policy nears its end

> [!receipt] How to determine the total expected profit from an alteration?
>To find the total expected profit from the alteration, add the two bits together 
>- ***Profit released:*** The left-hand side is like a surrender value.
> 	- This profit is determined by the method and basis used to calculate the policy value _before_ the alteration
> - ***Profit emerging:*** The right-hand side is like a new contract. 
> 	- This is the profit expected to arise over the _remaining term_ of the altered contract, determined by the basis used for the new terms
>

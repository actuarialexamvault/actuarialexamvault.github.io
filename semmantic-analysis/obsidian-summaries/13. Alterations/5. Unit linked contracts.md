
> [!trending-up] How do insurers treat alterations on unit linked contracts?
> - ***Paid-up status:*** When a policyholder stops paying premiums, the units generally remain attached to the contract. However, the insurer may deduct a penalty from the unit fund to recoup initial expenses that were expected to be covered by future unallocated premiums.
>- ***Flexible adjustments***: Many contracts (especially pensions and whole life) allow policyholders to increase or decrease premiums and change retirement dates.
>- ***Alteration penalties:*** Insurers will generally apply a penalty if the alteration reduces the EPV of future charges. However, for marketability reasons some companies may accept the loss as a cross-subsidy if the volume of alterations is small.

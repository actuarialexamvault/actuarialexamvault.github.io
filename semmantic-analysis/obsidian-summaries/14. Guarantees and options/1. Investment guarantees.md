
> [!question] What are the implications of offering an investment guarantee?
>- ***Investment risk***: the guarantee transfers investment risk from the policyholder to the insurance company. The primary implication is the risk that the **assets backing the policy will be insufficient to meet the guaranteed amount** at the time of the claim.
> - ***Investment strategy***: Insurers face a choice between investing to match the guarantee (which limits performance) or investing for higher returns.
> - ***Predictability***: guarantees that can be exercised at any time (like surrender value guarantees) are harder to manage than fixed maturity guarantees because the insurer doesn't know exactly when the assets will be needed.
> - ***Pricing***: deciding on the additional premium to cover the **cost of the guarantee**, usually requires sophisticated [[../../Definitions/Stochastic models|stochastic modelling]] to estimate.
> - ***Capital strain***: guarantees require higher supervisory reserves and solvency capital, which increases the [[../../Definitions/New Business Strain|initial capital strain]].

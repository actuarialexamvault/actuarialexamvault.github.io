
> [!skull] What are the implications of offering a mortality option?
> Offering a mortality option such as the right to increase cover or renew a policy without further medical evidence:
>- ***Anti-selection***: The primary risk is **selection against the office**, where policyholders in poor health are significantly more likely to exercise the option. 
>- ***Financial cost***: The cost is the difference between the standard premium charged and the higher premium that would have been charged under full underwriting. $$\{\text{proportion of lives exercising the option}\} \times \{\text{average health of lives exercising the option}\} $$
>- **Higher Capital strain**: reserving for mortality guarantees must account for both **expected higher mortality** of those exercising the option and the uncertainty regarding **future take up rates**.

> 
>|**Component**|**Control Detail**|
|---|---|


> [!steps] List the reasons for reinsuring
> - **C** – **Capital** (Raise capital or use the reinsurer's capital to reduce requirements)
  >  
>- **A** – **Assistance** (Product design, pricing assumptions, underwriting processes, claims management)
  >  
>- **P** – **Profit** (Increase profit, return, or risk-adjusted return on capital)
  >  
>- **I** – **Individual claims** (Limit the amount paid on any particular claim)
  >  
>- **T** – **Total claims** (Limit the total claims pay-out)
  >  
>- **A** – **Free Assets** (the level of free assets will influence the extent of reinsurance sought)
  >  
>- **L** – **Limit concentration** (Allow concentration of risks the cedant couldn't manage alone)
>..
>
>- **P** – **Parameter risk** Sharing
>- **R** – **Reduce fluctuations** (Reduce claim fluctuations year-on-year)
 >   
>- **I** – **Insurance parameter risk** (Reduce insurance parameter risk)
  >  
>- **S - Strain** Reduce new business strain
 >   
>- **KS** – **Keep Stability** (A mnemonic bridge to remember that the primary goal of the above is financial stability)

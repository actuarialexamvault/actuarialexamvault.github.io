
> [!question] How does reinsurance reduce parameter risk?
> Reinsurance reduces the risk that assumptions used in pricing or valuation are incorrect through two mechanisms:
> - ***Risk sharing***: By using quota share reinsurance, the insurer directly shares the financial impact of any mis-estimated parameters, as the reinsurer will absorb a proportional **share of the losses**
> - ***Technical expertise***: Reinsurers have access to broarder industry data and specialized knowledge in underwriting and product design. By assisting the insurer in setting more accurate initial assumptions, the reinsurer helps minimize the parameter risk before business is written.
>
>This is valuable for companies **entering new markets** or **launching new products** where **internal data is scarce**.

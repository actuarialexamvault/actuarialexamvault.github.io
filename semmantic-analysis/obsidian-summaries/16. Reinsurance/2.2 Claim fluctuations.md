
> [!question] How does reinsurance reduce claim pay-out fluctuations?
> Reinsurance reduces claim pay-out fluctuations by decreasing the variance of the insurer's total claim costs through two primary strategies.
> - ***Individual large claims***: By using **individual surplus reinsurance**, an insurer caps its exposure on any single policy. The reinsurer pays any portion of a claim exceeding a predetermined retention limit, ensuring that a few very **large policies** do not cause massive **spikes in total pay-outs**.
> - ***Aggregation of claims***: Catastrophe reinsurance addresses non-independent risks, similarly stop loss reinsurance caps the aggregate net loss for an entire portfolio over a year, providing a protection against a **general spike in claims** across all policies.
>
>These protections are vital for **smaller insurers** that do not have enough policies for random fluctuations to "smooth out" naturally through the law of large numbers.

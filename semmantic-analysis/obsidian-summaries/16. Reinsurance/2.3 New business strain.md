
> [!question] How does reinsurance reduce new business strain?
> Reinsurance reduces the initial capital strain cause by high initial costs and capital requirements through two mechanisms:
>- ***Reinsurance commissions/loans***: Under OTR or Fin Re, the reinsurer pays the insurer an initial reinsurance commission or loans that are contingent of the business written making a profit. These can be used to finance the initial costs.
>- ***Reduced reserving requirements***: By transferring a portion of the liability, the insurer is only required to establish supervisory reserves for the part of the risk it retains. Which lowers the amount of capital required at policy inception.

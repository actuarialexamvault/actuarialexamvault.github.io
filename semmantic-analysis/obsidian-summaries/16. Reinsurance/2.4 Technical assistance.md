
> [!question] In which areas might a reinsurer offer technical assistance to a cedant?
> - ***Underwriting***: Helping to assess complex or unusual risks and setting standards for accepting risks.
> - ***Product design:*** Providing guidance when an insurer is launching a new line of business or innovative features.
> - ***Pricing***: Assisting with the development of premium rates 
> - ***Systems design***: Offering expertise in creating the administrative and technological frameworks needed to manage policies.


> [!question] How does reinsurance reduce costs for the cedant?
> - ***Risk diversification***: Reinsurers typically have a much larger spread of risks, which reduces their exposure to random fluctuations and parameter risk. This allows them to include smaller risk margins in their premiums than a direct writer would need.
> - ***Regulatory solvency requirements***: Reinsurers may operate under jurisdictions with lower supervisory capital requirements, allowing them to price cover more cheaply than the cedant's internal cost of of capital.
> - ***Tax arbitrage***: reinsurers may operate under jurisdictions with more favourable tax regimes, allowing them to price cheaply than the cedant would have on its own.
>- ***Capital efficiency***: By transferring risk, the cedant reduces its own overall capital requirements, which reduces the cost of capital and leverages up the return on the remaining capital employed.


> [!question] What are the considerations before reinsuring?
> Before deciding whether and how to reinsure, an insurer evaluates several strategic and financial factors:
>- **Type of reinsurance**: The type of reinsurance (whether it is OTR, Risk premiums or excess of loss or Fin Re), will depend will depend on:
>	- The reason the insurance company is using reinsurance
>	- The reinsurance cost
>	- The type of insurance business for which the reinsurance is to be arranged
>- **Cost of Reinsurance**: Because reinsurers include their own profit margins and expenses, which reduce the insurer's absolute profit level. the insurer must balance security versus return. It must determine if the reduction in volatility justifies the reduced expected return.
>- **Retention Limits** must be set by considering the company's risk appetite, its level of free assets, and the distribution of sum assured across its portfolio. A company with higher free assets can generally afford a higher retention limit.
>- **Counterparty (Credit) Risk**: The insurer retains liability according to the contract, to pay benefits even if the reinsurer cannot meet claim payments (becomes insolvent)
>- **Alternatives**, such as holding a mortality fluctuation reserve may be more cost-effective than purchasing reinsurance.
>- **Legal Risks**: Finally the company should consider legal risk by ensuring treaties are legally watertight.

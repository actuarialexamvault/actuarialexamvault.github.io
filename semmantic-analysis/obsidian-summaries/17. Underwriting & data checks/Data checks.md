### **R – Reconciliation (B A M I)**

_Ensuring the data adds up and flows correctly._

- **B – Business movements:** Track New Business, Exits, and Persisting business.
- **A – Accounting tie-backs:** Do the claims in your data match the Revenue Account?
- **M – Movements data controls:** Checking system integrity, staff procedures, and independence of data sources.
- **I – Items reconciled:**  _Non-unitised:_ Contracts, Sum Assured, Premiums, Bonuses.
    - _Unitised:_ Contracts, Units (by fund), Premiums, Benefits.

---

### **C – Consistency Checks (A R U S)**

_Ensuring the relationships between data points make sense._

- **A – Averages reasonable:** Checking if average premiums or sum assureds look "normal."
- **R – Ratios sensible:** Is the Sum Assured ÷ Premium ratio stable over time?
- **U – Unit movements logical:** Do units bought/sold align with premiums/benefits?
- **S – Surplus consistency:** Do charge encashments align with expected surplus?

---

### **U – Unusual Values & Spot Checks (V D D P)**

_Hunting for the outliers and impossible entries._

- **V – Value extremes:** Zero or massive unit values.
- **D – Dates impossible:** Check for "future" start dates or 120-year-old policyholders.
- **D – Distribution checks:** Look for weird spikes (e.g., everyone born on Jan 1st).
- **P – Paper file checks:** The "reality check", comparing the system to the actual physical files.

---
### **S – Surplus & Embedded Value (D M)**

***The high-level reasonability test for the final profit result.***

An analysis of surplus shows the **sources** of the total profit.  

**Deviations (AvE)**: For items of experience , the profit will be based on the difference between **actual vs expected** experience (as per valuation basis). If any of the items appear to be **significantly** different from the prior valuation, then this could indicate a problem with the data.

**Movements**: Can you explain the movements that add up to the total profit (assets minus liabilities), if there is 'unexplained' profit/loss, this could indicate data errors.

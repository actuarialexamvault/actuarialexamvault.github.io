
> [!trajectories] List asset characteristics
> When selecting investments, an insurer evaluates assets based on six primary characteristics
> - Security
> - Yield/Return
> - Spread
> - Term
> - Expenses
> - Marketability
> - Tax
> - Currency

> [!trending-up] What do actuaries consider about the return of an asset?
> This is the most important characteristic of the asset, we need to think about:
> - How much is the expected return?
> - Is the return real or nominal?
> - How is the return split between income and capital redemption?
> - Is the **[[../../Definitions/Running yield]]** sufficient for the investor's needs?
> - What is the variance of the return?
> - How is the return affected by tax?
>

> [!solvency] What do actuaries have to consider regarding the security of an asset
>- Actuaries both the risk of default and the volatility of the asset's market values. 
>- It directly affects the insurers ability to meet its obligations to policyholders.
>- Generally assets with higher expected returns (like equities) also have higher variance/risk.

> [!money-tree] What do actuaries have to consider about the marketability of the asset?
> Some assets, like government bonds have low dealing costs and high liquidity, while others like property, are "chunky" expensive to administer, and difficult to sell quickly to meet obligations.
>


> [!lapse] How can an insurer monitor and improve persistency rates?
>- ***Monitor persistency rates*** by 
>	- distribution channel and 
>	- by the salesperson/broker concerned 
>	- updating pricing and valuation assumptions if lapse experience changes
>- ***Align sales incentives with persistency*** through:
>	- Lower initial commissions and higher renewal commission
>	- Clawing back initial commission on lapse
>	- Financial underwriting to ensure affordability
>- ***Identify systematic reasons***: 
>- ***Customer relationship***
>	- ***Reliable Payment methods***
>	- ***Policy design addresses customer needs***
>	- ***Professional training*** of sales staff reduces the incentive to over-sell 
>- ***Product design and flexibility***
>	- **Premium holidays**: allow policyholders to skip premiums for a time without the policy lapsing during financial hardship
>	- **Linked-claim periods**: [[../2. Health and care products/5.1.2 Income Protection (IP) contracts - Features#^e2e92b|See Income Protection (IP) contracts - Features]] 
>	- Capping premium increases: For reviewable products, capping annual can reduce lapses and prevent [[../../Definitions/Selective withdrawal|selective withdrawal]]
>	- **Fair alteration terms**: to prevent lapse and re-entry 
>	- **Surrender penalties**: to reduce [[../../Definitions/Investment selection|investment selection]] against the company
>- training sales staff
>- building brand and reputation

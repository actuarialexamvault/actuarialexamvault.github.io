
> [!gears] What is the role of the actuary in managing a life insurer's risks?
> - The actuary's primary role should be a regular programme of advising the directors of the nature and size of the risks faced by the company.
> - The key role is to identify, quantify and illustrate the risks and rewards of various courses of action
>- In the case of **sensitive risks** (those which the company is most vulnerable to) the actuary should model a range of scenarios to show the impact
> - It is essential to explain all actions that can be taken to reduce risks
> - Risk management strategies should be well documented, implemented and their effectiveness monitored continually
> - ERM is an integrated approach to risk management that looks at risks at an aggregate level which allows managers to understand concentrations of risk and the benefits of diversification.

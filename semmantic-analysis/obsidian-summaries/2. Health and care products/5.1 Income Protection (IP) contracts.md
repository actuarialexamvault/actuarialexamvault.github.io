
> [!definition] Definition
> Income Protection is a health and care insurance product designed to **replace a portion of the income** an insured individual would have earned if they become **incapacitated** (unable to work).
> - Claims **due to accident and/or illness**
> - Benefits will be paid on **temporary disability** (not only on permanent as is the case with lump sum disability cover)
> - The benefits (a regular annuity) are paid until the insured **recovers, dies or the policy term ends** (usually normal retirement age).
> - A number of **separate benefit periods** can occur without the policy ceasing
> - In some instances the regular income may be **commutable to a lump sum**
> - Benefits can **escalate** before claim payment commences
> - Benefits can also **escalate** once the claim is in payment
> - **Premiums** are usually level, but may also increase if the benefit is increasing
> - The benefit may include a **waiver of premium** benefit
> - The sum insured (annuity benefit) is set so as to **avoid over-insurance** (replacement ration <=100%)
> - Several **definitions of disability** are possible, for example:
> 	- Unable to do own occupation, any or reasonable occuation
> 	- Inability to perform certain activities of daily living
> 	- Functional assessment tests
> - A **partial benefit** is possible if the policyholder is able to return to work on a parttime basis
> - There is usually **no surrender of maturity benefit**

>[!list] Factors defining the benefit
>- Income level
>- Replacement ratio
>- Definition of disability
>- Waiting period
>- Deferred period
>- Linked-claims period
>- Escalation rate of benefits
>	- before claim
>	- in payment
>- Proportionate / rehabilitation benefit
>- Benefit expiry age / retirement age

>[!receipt] Pricing assumptions
>- Need both bestimates and margins to be included
>- Disability
>	- Inception rates
>	- recovery rates
>- Mortality
>	- Pre-claim
>	- During claim
>- Claim termination rates, which depend on
>	- recovery rates
>	- Mortality rates
>- Expense assumptions
>	- Initial (inc commission)
>	- on-going commission and renewal expenses
>	- expense inflation
>- Claim assessment
>	- at claim inception 
>	- subsequent re-assessments
>

>- [[Reasons for absence of surrender values for term assurances|No surrender value]] or maturity benefit.

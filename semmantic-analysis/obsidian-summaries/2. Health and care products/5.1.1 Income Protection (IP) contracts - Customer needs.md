
> [!INFO] [[Customer needs]]
> - **Financial Protection**:
> 	- loss of income for dependents 
> 	- standard of living, 
> 	- state benefits are unavailable or insufficient
>- ***Meeting financial obligations***:
>	- loan/mortgage payments 
>- ***Maintaining other Insurance policies***: 
>	- waiver of premium 
>- ***Rehabilitation assistance***: 
>	- finance recuperation needs, 
>	- personal support, advice and counselling  
>- ***Specific needs of employers and employees***:
>	- *Self-employed individuals*: 
>	- *Filling gaps in State and Employer provision*
>	- *Employers* use it for discretionary or statutory sick pay. 
>	- *Small professional practices*: *Locum Protection insurance* to fund the salary and employment costs of a temporary replacement if a partner becomes disabled
>- ***Peace of mind***: giving the psychological effect of knowing that you are protected.

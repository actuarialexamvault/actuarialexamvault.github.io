
> [!group] Group IP
> Group IP products are bought by employers wishing to provide benefits to their employees.
> - ***Statutory sickness pay***: employers use group IP schemes to insure their liability to pay **statutory sickness benefits**
> - ***Discretionary sickness pay***: employers may also use group IP to provide discretionary sickness/disability benefits to employees.
> - ***Administration***: Alternatively, the employer may only administer the scheme with each employee paying their own premiums through salary deductions.

> [!receipt] Level of benefits
> - **proportion of salary gross/net of tax**
> - Cover may also include pension contributions on behalf of the member
> - Benefits are allowed to escalate whilst in claim in order to maintain the claimants **standard of living**.

> [!time] Continuation option
> A continuation option may be offered which allows the employee to effect an individual policy without providing evidence of health when he or she leaves the service of the employer. This creates an [[Anti-selection]] risk.

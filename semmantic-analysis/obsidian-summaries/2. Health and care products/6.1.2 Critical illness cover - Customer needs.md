
> [!info] Customer needs
> CI is a pure protection product that can be used to meet the following needs:
> - ***Income replacement***: While CI provides a lump sum, this benefit can be used to provide an income via an annuity if the individual is unable to work as a result of their illness
> - ***Debt repayment***: The benefit can be used to repay a mortgage or other loans, providing financial security when the policyholder's health is compromised.
> - ***Medical and treatment costs***: CI payouts can fund surgery or other expensive medical treatments. The limitation is that the benefit is a fixed monetary amount rather than an indemnity for the actual costs of medical care.
> - ***Keyman Cover***: Business partners can use CI policies to fund the buyout of a stake in a partnership should one partner suffer a critical illness.
> - ***Lifestyle changes***: The payout enables policyholders to fund a change of lifestyle to improve their health, such a moving to a less stressful and lower paid job following a heart attack.
> - ***Recuperation and medical aids***: Benefits used to fund recuperation after an illness or the installation of medical equipment in the home.
> - ***Financial planning and wealth transfer***: Because CI cover provides a lump sum that is not designed to indemnify medical costs, the policyholder has the flexibility to use the funds for an purpose. e.g. The lump sum can also be used to offset the impact of tax-related obligations such as death duties or inheritance tax.

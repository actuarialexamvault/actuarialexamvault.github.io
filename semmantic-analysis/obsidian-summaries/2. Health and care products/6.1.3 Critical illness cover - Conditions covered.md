
> [!info] Criteria for Insurability
> For a condition to be suitable for inclusion in a CI policy, it must meet three primary characteristics
> - it must be **perceived by the public** as **serious** and **occur with relative frequency**
> - It must be **clearly defined** so that **no ambiguity** exists during the claims process
> - There must be **sufficient data available** to price the benefit accurately

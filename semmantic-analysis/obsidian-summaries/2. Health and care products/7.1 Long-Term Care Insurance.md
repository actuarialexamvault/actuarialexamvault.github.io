#actuarial #f102 #life_insurance #definitions

> [!definition] Definition
> 
> This type of insurance can be used to provide **protection against the risk of needing either home or nursing-home care** as an elderly person, ie post-retirement. It is intended for those whose health is not expected to improve.

> [!family] Benefits 
>- ***Indemnity vs Cash***: The contract could pay for all the costs of care throughout the remainder of life (an [[Indemnity]] contract), or could provide a cash lump sum or annuity to contribute towards the costs of care.
>- ***Death and paid-up benefits***: Some plans offer a death benefit a paid-up benefit may also be available for regular premium policies.
>- ***Claim triggers***: Benefits are usually triggered by a defined level of disability, measured by the failure of a specified number (3/4) of [[Activities of Daily Living (ADLs)|ADL's]] or alternative measures of impairment.
>- **Exclusions**: for temporary disability for recuperation, for example after surgery (only genuine incapacity that is permanent)
>- **Deferred periods** to ensure that incapacity is permanent
>

## LTCI Cash Benefit vs Indemnity Benefit

| Option                | Main advantages                                                                                                                         | Main disadvantages                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cash benefit**      | Simpler, cheaper, flexible, benefit amount known in advance, lower claims administration.                                               | May not cover actual care costs, exposed to care-cost inflation, possible over-insurance or under-insurance.                                             |
| **Indemnity benefit** | Better matches actual cost of care, gives stronger protection against care-cost uncertainty, reduces dissatisfaction if costs are high. | More expensive, more complex to administer, more guarantees, more scope for claims disputes, usually subject to limits or caps, greater risk to insurer. |

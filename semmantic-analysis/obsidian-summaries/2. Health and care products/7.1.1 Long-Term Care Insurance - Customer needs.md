
> [!info] Customer needs
> The primary need met by LTCI is financial protection against the risk of having insufficient funds to pay for care in old age. 
> - ***Maintaining independence (avoiding burden)***: fulfils the desire to avoid dependence on the care from family or friends (informal care).
> - ***Asset protection***: protects personal assets (such as a home) from being used to pay for care.
> - ***Protection for elderly relatives***: Policies may be purchased by individuals who feel responsible for the welfare of elderly relatives or friends.
> - ***Specialized support***: LTCI often provides non-financial benefits that meet practical needs, such as assistive devices e.g. gra rails, lifts and personal alarms linked to medical providers. 
> - ***Risk of disability***: addresses the additional costs of data-to-day living caused by disability (immediate needs plan for those already in failing health), pre-funded plans can be purchased by healthy people to protect against the future risk of disability.

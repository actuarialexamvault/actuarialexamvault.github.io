
> [!abstract] What are the reasons we should monitor the actual experience of a life insurance company?
> Acronym: M.U.S.I.C
>- **M** – **Management Information:** Providing data for remuneration schemes, rewards, and identifying which activities are actually profitable.
>
>- **U** – **Update Assumptions:** Refining models and pricing assumptions based on how reality is unfolding.
  > 
>- **S** – **Surplus Analysis:** Investigating the financial impact of differences between actual and expected results (Analysis of Surplus).
>- **I** – **Identify Adverse Trends:** Spotting adverse patterns early so you can take swift corrective action.
>
>- **C** – **Calculate Asset Shares:** Determining earned asset shares, which are essential for setting fair bonus levels.

> [!EXAMPLE] What corrective actions might be taken as a result of identifying adverse trends in experience?
> - Re-pricing products
> - Re-design products
> - change the investment strategy
> - change the sales strategy
> 	- distribution channels
> 	- commission structures
> - change the reinsurance strategy
> - change the underwriting process
> - change the profit distribution strategy e.g. bonus rates
> - re-organise the workforce and IT systems to make more efficient use of expensive resources

> [!IMPORTANT] What other information can be provided to management through monitoring?
> By monitoring experience the management of a company may be able to identify ways in which it can make its operation more profitable, such as identifying:
> - profitable products
> - profitable sales channels or agents
> - profitable markets
> - efficient sections of the business
> - successful investment strategies

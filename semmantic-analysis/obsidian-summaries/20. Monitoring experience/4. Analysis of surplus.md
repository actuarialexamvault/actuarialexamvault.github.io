
> [!gears] Why would an insurance company want to perform an analysis of surplus?
> ACRONYM: D.I.S.C.O.R
> 
>**Memory Aid:** If the **D.I.S.C.O.R.** (Discord) between your expected and actual surplus is too high, you know exactly which of these six areas to investigate.
>
>
> |**Letter**|**Component**|**Description**|
|---|---|---|
|**D**|**D**ivergences|Quantify the **financial impact** of differences in actual vs expected experience.|
|**I**|**I**nformation|Give **management information** on trends in experience to guide strategic management decisions.|
|**S**|**S**train|Measuring the **initial strain** of writing new policies.|
|**C**|**C**ompliance|Satisfying the "Analysis of Change" requirements under frameworks like IFRS 17 or SAM.|
|**O**|**O**nce-off|Distinguishing between sustainable and **one-off surplus** to inform decisions on **profit distribution** to with-profit policyholders.|
|**R**|**R**econcile|Acting as a "sanity check" for the integrity of **data and models** used in valuation processes.|

> [!receipt] What is valuation surplus and surplus arising?
> The surplus at any point is the value of the difference between assets less liabilities, which is the [[../../Definitions/Free Assets|free assets]]. The surplus arising within a particular year is the change in this value, which is the [[../7. Modelling/13.2 Model components#^2a79a2|profit]] for the period. The liabilities in this case include both the supervisory reserves and the solvency capital requirement. 


> [!compare] What are the sources of surplus?
>Surplus / loss arises due to the difference between actual experience and valuation assumptions for: 
>- Investment return 
>- mortality (and other contingencies)
>- expenses
> - withdrawal rates
> 
> 

> [!definition] How does the choice of valuation basis affect the analysis of surplus?
>- **Prudent basis**: We expect to see a positive surplus arising representing the release of margins. Alarm bells should ring if any item is negative because it means experience was worse than the most cautious estimation
>- **Best estimate**: If the analysis is done on an internal realistic basis, we would expect to see negatives just as often as positives, especially if the portfolio of business is small. A negative result is not a big worry, but would be worrisome if were very large or if it is repeated year after year.

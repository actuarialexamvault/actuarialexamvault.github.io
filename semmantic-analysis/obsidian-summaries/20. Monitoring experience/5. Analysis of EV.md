
> [!question] What are the reasons for performing an analysis of EV?
> ACRONYM: VAMPER
> 
> |**Letter**|**Component**|**Why it is Crucial**|
|---|---|---|
|**V**|**Validate**|validate the calculations, assumptions and data used in the EV calculation.|
|**A**|**Accounts**|Investors and analysts need the "Value of New Business" (VNB) to judge growth.|
|**M**|**Management**|Helps leadership see where value is being created (or destroyed) in the portfolio.|
|**P**|**Parent**|Crucial for groups where a subsidiary’s EV is a major asset on the parent’s balance sheet.|
|**E**|**Executive**|Aligns management incentives with long-term value creation rather than short-term sales.|
|**R**|**Reconcile**| Reconcile the values for successive years, Explains the "Analysis of Change"—how you got from last year's EV to this year's.|


> [!summary] Product & Pricing (The "What")
> Actions following experience analysis related to product and pricing:
>
> - **(a) Updating the pricing basis:** Adjusting premiums for new business based on A/E trends.
> - **(b) Revising product design:** Removing poorly used or high-risk options/benefits.
> - **(c) Changing product mix:** Shifting focus away from unprofitable lines toward successful ones.
> - **(d) Revising underwriting:** Increasing evidence requirements if mortality/morbidity is too high.
> - **(e) Revising reinsurance:** Using risk transfer to smooth volatile experience.
> - **(i) Improving policy wording:** Reducing legal risk and customer complaints through clarity.

> [!summary] Operational & Distribution (The "How")
> Actions focusing on business efficiency, sales quality, and long-term customer value:
>
> - **(f) Retention activity:** Investing in persistency to keep policies on the books.
> - **(g) Marketing & Target Market:** Redirecting spend to more successful channels or segments.
> - **(h) Sales & Commission:** Fixing broker issues, premium collection delays, or clawback terms.
> - **(j) Staffing resources:** Scaling human capital to match business volumes and competence needs.

> [!summary] Actuarial & Risk Management (The "Math")
> Actions involving technical models and the governance that oversees them:
>
> - **(l) Improving actuarial models:** Ensuring rating factors in pricing models reflect significant factors identified in the analysis.
> - **(o) Updating the reserving basis:** Aligning the valuation basis with actual past experience.
> - **(r) Risk management governance:** Using MI to change how decisions are made (e.g., meeting frequency).
> - **(k) Systems & Data:** Upgrading infrastructure to allow for more sophisticated future analysis.

> [!summary] Capital & Investment (The "Money")
> Actions dealing with the balance sheet, the assets, and the distribution of value:
>
> - **(m) Changing investment strategy:** Fixing ALM mismatches if investment surplus is volatile.
> - **(n) With-profits distribution:** Adjusting the split between reversionary and terminal bonuses.
> - **(p) Raising capital:** Seeking new funds if surplus is negative or solvency is threatened.
> - **(q) Capital allocation:** Redirecting capital to lines of business with higher risk-adjusted returns.

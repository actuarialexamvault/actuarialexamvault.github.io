
> [!ABSTRACT] What is asset share
> - The asset share of any policy is the **accumulation** of all premiums received rolled up with interest, less expenses and the cost of claims. (similar to the [[Gross premium retrospective reserve]])

> [!important] Asset share is the accumulation of **monies in** less **monies out**.

>[!in-flow] Monies In (Income):    
>- **Premiums paid** by the policyholder.
>- **Actual investment return**, which includes both investment income and unrealized capital appreciation.
>- **Share of profits from other business**, such as profits from without-profits business that are distributed to the with-profits fund.

> [!out-flow] Monies Out (Deductions):
>- **Commissions and expenses**, including initial acquisition costs, renewal costs, and a share of the company's overheads (typically net of tax).
>- **The cost of claims**, representing the cost of providing death benefits, guarantees, or options in excess of the asset share.
>- **Tax on investment income, net of any expense relief**, specifically tax paid on investment income and reserves held for future tax liabilities.
>- **Transfers of profit to shareholders** (in proprietary companies).
>- **Cost of capital**, which are deductions made to compensate the company for the cost of capital used to support the policy's initial "new business strain".
>- **Contributions to free assets**, which are used to support the smoothing of bonuses and provide the company with greater investment flexibility.

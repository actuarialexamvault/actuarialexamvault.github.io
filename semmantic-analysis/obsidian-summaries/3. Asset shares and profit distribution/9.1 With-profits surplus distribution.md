
> [!abstract] Delaying profit distribution
> Delaying the distribution of profit helps insurers to **limit risk exposure** and increase **financial security**. 
> 
> It reduces the probability of insolvency by:
> - Avoiding the creation of guaranteed liabilities
> - Increasing free assets
> 
The benefits are
>-  Investment freedom (to more volatile assets)
> - Financing new business and growth
> 
> The company delays profit distribution by using:
> - Terminal bonuses
> - Smoothing 

> [!important] Bonus distribution considerations
> - ***Policyholder reasonable expectations*** regarding level and volatility of bonuses developed from:
> 	- past practice of the company
> 	- marketing material, point of sale disclosure
> 	- company communication regarding bonus declarations
> 	- market practice
> 	- inflation, expectations of real returns
> - ***Equitable*** between different **categories** and **generations** of policyholders
> - Not threaten the:
> 	- future business plans
> 	- investment strategy
> 	- or solvency of the company
> - ***Smoothing***: Dampening the volatility of investment returns so that payouts don't fluctuate wildly year-on-year.
> - **Competitiveness:** Ensuring bonus rates are attractive enough to bring in new business.
> - **Communication and reputation**: Clear and careful communication around bonus declarations otherwise there is a risk if:
> 	- policyholder complaints
> 	- reputational damage
> 	- legal or regulatory consequences
> 	- loss of trust
>


> [!info] To buy or not to buy?
> 
> - ***Lack of awareness***: Many consumers are simply unaware that they need a life insurance product until its too late, or they operate under the belief that negative events won't happen to them.
> - ***Information imbalance***: Insurers possess full information regarding charges and competitiveness, while consumers have very little.
> - ***Usefulness of benefits***: in the long run a life insurer will run a risk to profit if it does not sell product with useful benefits to consumers
> - ***Need for education***: consumers need to be educated and informed about the benefits of life insurance and the company's products

> [!caution] Risks to the insurer
> The insurer runs the risk that a policy is sold which does not meet the agreed needs of a policyholder, which can ultimately lead to:
> - ***[[Withdrawal (Persistency) risk]]: ***If consumers are persuaded to buy products that do not meet their actual needs
> - ***[[Reputational risk]]*** due to a mismatch between consumer expectations and actual product performance (often due to poor documentation or lack of clarity)
> - Legal risk: if the business and legal environment enforces meeting [[Policyholders’ Reasonable Expectations (PRE)|PRE]] the insurer risks having clauses voided or being forced to pay compensation


> [!example] Main distribution channels
> The main distribution channels used by life insurance companies are:
> - Independent intermediaries
> - Tied Agents
> - Own sales force
> - Direct marketing
> 
>Each have distinct **effect on the sales process** and the resulting **risks to the insurer**.

^b15fc8

> [!group] Independent intermediaries
> ***IFAs & Brokers***: These independent entities select the best contracts for their clients from the entire market. Selling is often client-initiated as intermediaries tend to reach more financially sophisticated and wealthier clients who proactively seek advice for complex financial needs.

> [!tied] Tied Agents
>- These are agents are linked to one or few insurers and
>- They benefit from access to familiar customer bases (existing bank clients), making the sales process easier through established relationships. 
>- They only sell the products of affiliated insurance companies. 
>- Legally they work on behalf of the insurance company without being employees, whereas independent intermediaries act on behalf of the client.
>- They often operate as employees of banks or other financial institutions. 
>- Tied agents are renumerated in the form of commission payments or by salary plus bonuses

>[!call] Own salesforce
>- These guys are employees of the life insurance company and only sell the products of that company
>- They may be renumerated by commission or salary or a mixture of both
>- They usually initiate the sale, making use of client lists or leads.
>- They rely on **building rapport** with clients to encourage repeat business and cross-selling across the insurer's full product range

> [!in-bound] Direct marketing
> This channel includes mailshots, telephone, the internet and press advertising.
> - It is **company-initiated** and requires **simple product designs** (like term assurance) because the medium does not allow for the extensive explanation or education required for complex products.

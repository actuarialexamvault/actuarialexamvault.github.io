
> [!abstract] The state of the economy
> The state of the economy in which an insurer operates will carry risks for the insurer by influencing the:
> - ***Availability of asset types***: the available assets and their short and long term expected yields will influence **investment strategy and interest rate assumptions**.  From the **consumers point of view**, insurance products may be seen as more or less attractive when compared with other **available investment options** e.g. bank accounts, unit trusts, shares or not investing at all
> - ***Volatility of investment markets***: e.g. in developing markets, will tend to increase the cost of capital for insurers (higher capital requirements), so increasing premiums, and can also affect the demand for life insurance products.

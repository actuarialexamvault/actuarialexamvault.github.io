
> [!danger] Risks from counterparties
> ounterparty risk arises when an entity with which the insurer has an agreement defaults on its obligations or performs to an **unacceptable standard**.
>
>• **Reinsurance Default:** This is a critical **credit risk** because the insurer remains legally liable to the policyholder even if the reinsurer fails to pay its share of a claim.
>
>• **Outsourcing Failures:** Insurers often outsource functions like IT, investment management, and policy administration. Poor service quality from these counterparties can lead to **increased expenses, policyholder complaints**, and the need for costly corrective measures.
>
>• **Investment Defaults:** The insurer faces risk if the issuers of **corporate bonds or loans** fail to make coupon payments or return capital.
>
>• **Treaty and Operational Risk:** Risk arises if **reinsurance treaties are not finalized or signed**, potentially leading to disputes over recoveries

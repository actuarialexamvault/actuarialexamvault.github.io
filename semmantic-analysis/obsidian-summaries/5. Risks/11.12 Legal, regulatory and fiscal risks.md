> [!caution] Legal, regulatory and fiscal risks
> Future changes to any of these can adversely affect the insurer and/or its policyholders.
> See [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/4. External environment/10.5 Legal Environment|10.5 Legal Environment]] and [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/4. External environment/10.6 The tax regime#^0c3365|10.6 The fiscal regime]] for details.

> [!summary] Key legal, regulatory and fiscal risks
> Legal, regulatory and fiscal risks arise from changes to the external legal, regulatory or tax environment that affect the insurer or its policyholders. These include:
>
> - **Regulatory changes**: Changes to solvency requirements, reserving rules, product approval processes or conduct of business regulations may require product redesign, additional capital or changes to business practices.
> - **Legislative changes**: Changes to legislation affecting policy wording, benefit taxation, underwriting practices or claims definitions may alter the insurer's liability or policyholder behaviour.
> - **Fiscal changes**: Changes to tax treatment of premiums, benefits, investment income or reserves may affect the attractiveness and profitability of products.
> - **Compliance risk**: Failure to comply with current regulations may result in fines, reputational damage or forced changes to business practices.
> - **Retroactive risk**: Regulations or court decisions may be applied retrospectively, affecting existing in-force business.

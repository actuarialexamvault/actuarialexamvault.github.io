
> [!question] Risk and decision-making
> Risk of loss has to be measured in terms of the effect it may have on the **aims of the company**. These aims are:
> - to maximise profits, and/or
> - to maximise the return on capital
>
>Risk must also be assessed in relation to:
>- the company's **capital** and other resources
>- its impact on supervisory **solvency**
>- the cost of failing to meet any other **legislative requirements**
>
>The insurance company will make its strategic decisions by balancing the trade of between risk and return, which could be expressed as"
>- finding the appropriate **risk-return** combination, or
>- maximising expected return subject to an acceptable degree of risk.

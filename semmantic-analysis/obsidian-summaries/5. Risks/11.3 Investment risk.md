
> [!trending-down] Components of investment risk
>- ***Model risk***: there is a danger that the chosen mathematical model for future investment returns (e.g. the probability distribution for equity returns) is an **inappropriate representation of reality**.
>- ***Parameter risk***: even with an appropriate model, there is the risk that the **specific values** used to parameterise the model (such as the **mean return, variance or correlations** between asset classes) might be **incorrectly estimated**.
>- ***Random fluctuations***: future investment performance may not correspond exactly with the model or parameters adopted, even if those inputs are judged to be appropriate. 

> [!fail] Mismatching risk
> This involves a discrepancy between the **discounted mean term (duration)** of the assets and the liabilities
>- ***Reinvestment risk***: If assets have a shorter duration than liabilities, proceeds are available earlier than required and must be **reinvested at unknown future rates**. If interest rates fall, the insurer may be unable to secure the yields needed to meet long-term guarantees
>- ***Capital values risk***: If assets have a longer duration than liabilities, the insurer may be forced to sell assets to meet claims. If interest rates rise, the market value of the assets will fall more than the value of the liabilities, potentially causing insolvency.

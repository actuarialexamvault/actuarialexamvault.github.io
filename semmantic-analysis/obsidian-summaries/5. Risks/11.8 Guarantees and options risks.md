> [!trajectories] Parameter and model risk
> To offer a guarantee or option, actuaries must estimate its future cost:
> - ***Model risk***: The framework used to calculate the cost may be an over-simplification of reality. For example using a [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Definitions/Deterministic models|deterministic model]] to value an investment guarantee might suggest a zero cost of the best estimate/mean return is high, whereas a [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Definitions/Stochastic models|stochastic model]] would reveal a positive cost by accounting for the variability of investment returns.
> - ***Parameter risk***: even if the model is appropriate, the specific values used to calibrate the model (such as future interest rates or mortality improvements) may be incorrectly estimated.

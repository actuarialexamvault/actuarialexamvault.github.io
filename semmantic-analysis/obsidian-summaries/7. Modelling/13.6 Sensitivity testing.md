
> [!trajectories] Sensitivity testing
> Sensitivity tests show the vulnerability of the results to changes in assumptions (or unexpected future events).
> 
> Outcomes are analysed for sensitivity to variations in:
> - model point assumptions (volumes & business mix)
> - parameter values
> 
> Sensitivity testing, on a deterministic basis, can be used to determine the margins that may be necessary in a basis.
> 
> Such margins in each parameter can be a way of allowing for risk in a pricing model as an alternative to risk margins in the discount rate.
> 
> Sensitivity analysis can help to determine the variance of profit, or of the return on capital, where stochastic approaches are not viable.

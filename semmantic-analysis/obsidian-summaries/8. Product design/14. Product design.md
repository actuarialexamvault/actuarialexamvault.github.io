
> [!question] Why might an actuary be looking at a possible new product design?
> - identifying gaps in the product range
> - responding to external changes
> 	- legislative and fiscal regime changes
> 	- education and awareness which increases demand for certain products
> 	- new financial instruments
> - fixing inadequacies in existing products
> 	- loss making products or insufficient profitability
> 	- inefficient capital use
> 	- outdated pricing bases
> - maintaining competitiveness and marketability
> - risk management

> 
>|**Letter**|**Factor**|**Key Considerations**|
|---|---|---|
|**C**|**Competitiveness**|Pricing and features relative to the rest of the market.|
|**A**|**Administration**|How easily the product fits into current IT systems and workflows.|
|**M**|**Marketability**|Whether the product is easy to explain and attractive to distributors.|
|**P**|**Profitability**|Ensuring the product meets the required profit criteria or hurdle rate.|
|**-**|||
|**S**|**Sensitivity**|How vulnerable the profit is to changes in assumptions (e.g., mortality, interest).|
|**P**|**Product consistency**|Consistency with existing business and brand expectations.|
|**R**|**Regulatory**|Meeting all legal, tax, and capital adequacy requirements.|
|**I**|**Initial Capital**|The financing required for initial capital strain and setup costs.|
|**N**|**Needs**|How well the product addresses the specific needs of the customer.|
|**G**|**Guarantees**|The cost of providing guarantees and the impact on risk capital.|
|**S**|**Subsidies**|Avoiding or managing cross-subsidies between different policyholder groups.|


> [!caution] Regulatory requirements
> Regulatory environments are **significant drivers of product design**, primarily aimed at protecting the interests of policyholders and ensuring the insurer remains solvent.
>
>Restricts can apply to:  **TRUST CARDS**
>
>|**Letter**|**Regulation Category**|**Specific Details**|
|---|---|---|
|**T**|**Terms**|Requirements for policy terms and conditions.|
|**R**|**Rates**|Restrictions on premium rates or charges for specific contracts.|
|**U**|**Underwriting**|Limits on the ability to underwrite or differentiate (e.g., by gender).|
|**S**|**Statutory Roles**|Requirement for reserved roles (e.g., Statutory Actuary sign-off).|
|**T**|**TCF**|The overarching "Treating Customers Fairly" requirement.|
|**-**|||
|**C**|**Contracts**|Restrictions on the types of contracts permitted to be sold.|
|**A**|**Amount**|Constraints on business volume via reserves and solvency margins.|
|**R**|**Reinsurance**|Restrictions on what counts as permissible reinsurance.|
|**D**|**Distribution**|The "Big Five": Channels, Procedures, Info, Qualifications, Commission.|
|**S**|**Solvency**|Minimum solvency margins and capital requirements.|
>

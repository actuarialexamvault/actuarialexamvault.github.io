
> [!important] Marketability
> Speaks the **attractiveness** of the product in the market:
> - ***Customer needs***: a product must be designed to meet specific [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Definitions/Customer needs|financial needs]]. 
> - ***Affordability***: premiums must be perceived as reasonable relative to benefits provided.
> - ***Expectations***: design choices must respect traditional practices e.g. level premiums 
> - ***Utility***: maximizing the [[../../Definitions/Utility|Utility]] of a product to the consumer.
> - ***Distribution channels***: the [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/4. External environment/10.2 Distribution channels|distribution channel]] will have a major influence on product design
> - **Volume of new business**: Higher volumes could reduce per-policy fixed expense loadings
> 
> Insurers face **[[../../Definitions/Marketing risk|market risk]]** if they fail to manage policyholders expectations in the design phase due to [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Definitions/Reputational risk|reputation risk]] which could lead to loss of future sales.

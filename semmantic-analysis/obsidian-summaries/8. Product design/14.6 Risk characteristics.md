
> [!solvency] Accepting risk
> The acceptability of a products design and risk profile depends on the company's willingness and ability to absorb risk internally or its capacity to transfer that risk. 
> 
> If a company faces a large [[../../Definitions/Parameter risk|parameter risk]] (such as uncertain mortality rates in a new market), it may adjust the product design in several ways:
> - **Offering reviewable terms** instead of long term guarantees, which allows the insurer to adjust charges if experience deteriorates
> - **Offering a unit-linked version** of the product, which passes on the investment risk to the policyholder
> - **Reinsure** a large part of the risk to limit its exposure
> - **Risk margins**: incorporate ample risk margins in the premium rates to protect against adverse experience.
> - **Rider benefits**: offer the contract as an additional rider benefit rather than a standalone. 

> [!explainer] [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Explainers/Why rider benefits are less risky than standalone versions]]

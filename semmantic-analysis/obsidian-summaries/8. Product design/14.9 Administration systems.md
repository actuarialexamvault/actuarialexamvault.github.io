> [!caution] Administration systems
> Administration systems are a primary practical constraint because **the system requirements of a new product may limit the benefits provided or the charging structure adopted.**

 >[!info] Capacity for complexity
> Administration systems must be robust enough to maintain detailed policy and claims files for complex product designs, such as recording the specific **start dates**, **end dates** and **reasons** for claims. 
> 
> Systems must be capable of handling the specific mechanics of a product; for example, Income Protection (IP) and Long-Term Care Insurance (LTCI).
> 
>If a company’s computer system cannot cope with a feature, such as a waiver of premium option, that feature may be excluded unless the cost of upgrading the system is justified by its importance.

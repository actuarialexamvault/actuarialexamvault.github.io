
> [!info] Setting assumptions
> An insurer will make assumptions about future events to assess the eventual costs of its liabilities. Risk arises because these assumptions are made about an uncertain future, and reality will almost inevitably turn out differently from what was expected. Assumptions, cannot necessarily be matched by assets which protect the insurer from reality turning out differently from that assumed. 
> 
> When the actual experience deviates from the assumptions, the decisions a company made regarding pricing, investment or reserving may no longer be appropriate, leading to financial losses or threats to solvency.

> [!caution] Parameter risk
> Assumptions create [[../../../Definitions/Parameter risk|Parameter risk]], and setting parameters as correctly as possible is the best way of controlling parameter risk.

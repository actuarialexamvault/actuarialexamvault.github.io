
> [!cycle] The five-step framework
> 1. ***Determine the best estimate***: Analyse past experience (**historical data**) to establish best-estimate parameters based on historical demographic, commercial and economic conditions
> 
> 2. ***Adjust for future expectations***: Consider how the future economic and commercial environment will differ from the past. Use the historical parameters as a starting point and adjust them for expected future changes.
> 3. **Balance Credibility and Judgment:** Balance the reliance on data with professional judgment based on the relevance and predictability of the parameter
> 4. ***Incorporate margins for prudence***: Adjust best estimates to include a margin for prudence based on the purpose of the investigation and the degree of risk


> [!abstract] Setting expense and commission assumptions involves categorizing costs into **initial**, **renewal** and **termination** expenses.

> [!receipt] Type of expenses and commissions
> 
**Expenses**
>- ***Initial expenses***: acquisition costs such as marketing, medical underwriting and the technical setup of the policy in the company's systems.
> - ***Renewal expenses***: ongoing administration, investment management, and the cost of regular communications e.g. telephone support.
> - ***Termination expenses***: administrative costs incurred when a policy ends due to death, maturity or surrender
>
>***Commissions***:
> - ***Initial commission***: a percentage of the first year's premium
> - ***Renewal commission***: a percentage of each subsequent premium paid to reward the [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Summary/4. External environment/10.2 Distribution channels#^b15fc8|sales channel]].

> [!caution] Fixed vs Marginal costs
> In the pricing basis the expense loadings must be sufficient to cover all [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Definitions/Marginal costs|marginal costs]] and include a contribution toward fixed [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Definitions/Overheads|overheads]].  

> [!info] Allocating per-policy expenses
> - ***Policy fee***: A float monetary addition to the premium designed to cover per-policy administrative costs.
> - ***Proportional loadings***: Expenses like commissions or investment management are often assumed as a percentage of the premium, sum assured or the fund value
> - ***Sum assured differentials***: Different premium rates or charges may be set of different bands of policy size to better reflect the true cost of administration. 

> [!Example] Loading expenses to premiums
>|Expense Type|Loading Method|
|---|---|
|Initial acquisition (e.g., initial commission)|**Percentage of the first year’s premium**.|
|Initial medical underwriting|Combination of a **flat average cost per policy** and a **percentage of the benefit size** in the first year.|
|Initial administration|Combination of a **flat per-policy cost** and a **percentage of the benefit size** in the first year.|
|Renewal administration|**Flat average cost per policy** (adjusted for inflation) occurring from the second year onwards.|
|Renewal reward to sales channel (e.g., renewal commission)|**Percentage of premiums** paid from the second year onwards.|
|Investment expenses|**Percentage of reserves**, typically implemented as a **deduction from the interest rate**.|
|Withdrawal / paid-up expenses|Usually **no premium loading**; these costs are typically covered when setting the policy's **discontinuance terms** e.g. through surrender penalties or reduced benefit.|
|Claim / maturity administration (termination expenses)|**Fixed per-policy cost** (adjusted for inflation) at the time the policy terminates.|
|Contribution to overheads|Generally allowed for as a **proportion (percentage) of the premium**.|

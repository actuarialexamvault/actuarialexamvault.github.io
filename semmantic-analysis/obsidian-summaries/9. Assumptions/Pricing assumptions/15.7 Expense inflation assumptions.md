
> [!trending-up] Expense inflation
> Assumptions for future renewal and termination expenses must explicitly allow **inflation**. This will likely be linked to wage and salary inflation rather than retail price inflation. It must also be [[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Explainers/The link between inflation and investment returns|consistent with the investment return assumption]]. 

> [!list]  Data sources
> Actuaries look at publicly available data to determine base expectations, including:
> - **Consumer Price Index (CPI)**: Used for general price inflation
> - **Quarterly Employment Statistics (QES)**: published by StatsSA  e.g. by industry
> - **Average Monthly Earnings**: derived from QES, can be used to track earnings inflation
> - **MPC Forecasts** for short term forecasts 2-3 years
> - **Company experience**: recent actual internal data 
> - **Industry wide data** where relevant

> [!trajectories] Market-based approximation
> The **[[ASSA/F102 Life Insurance Principles/Explainers/The link between inflation and investment returns|differential between the return on government fixed-interest securities and government index-linked securities]]** provides a '*break-even*' inflation rate that is an indicator for what investors expect inflation to be over the term of the bods. However this is distorted by the **inflation risk premium**.

> [!solvency] The principle of consistency
> The inflation assumption cannot be set in isolation, it must maintain a **realistic long-term relationship** with the investment return assumption. If a model assumes higher inflation rates, it must generally assume a corresponding level of investment return (i.e. in the discount used to value liabilities) to remain consistent with the underlying economic environment.

> [!info] Deterministic vs Stochastic approaches
> In deterministic models, a **fixed annual rate** is chosen based on best estimates. In stochastic models inflation is often a **random variable** that drives the model, with investment returns and other parameters **dynamically linked** to the stochastically generated inflation rate.

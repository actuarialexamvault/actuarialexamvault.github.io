
> [!cycle] Methodology for setting withdrawal assumptions
>- ***Best estimate***: assumptions should ideally be based on an analysis of the company's own recent **historical experience** for the specific contract or a similar class of business. If internal data is inadequate, industry-wide experience may be used as a starting point. 
>- ***Adjustments***: Professional judgement is used to adjust historical data to reflect the expected future experience of new policyholders, particularly if there have been changes in:
>	- **Target markets** or the level of client sophistication
>	- **Distribution channels** or sales tactics e.g. aggressive sales pressure
>	- **Policy benefits**, such as changes in bonus rates or surrender value scales
>- **Balance credibility and judgement**: The extend of reliance on data versus judgement depends on the relevance, volume and predictability of the available information.
>- **Incorporating margins** for unpredictable economic and commercial factors, to protect the insurer against underestimating withdrawals. using judgement to assess the uncertainty.
